# NPCs and dialogue

- `game/npc/` owns ordinary villager schedules, movement and greetings. Generate `npc_catalogue.json` offline with `tools/import_npc_schedules.py` from the source cue table, native area routes and packed ANI catalogue. Use the source map/place pairs, weekday/season schedule variants and one shared villager scene. Story-dependent actors and cue actions are deferred. Anchor the 0.2 m grid at the visible soil edge (-4.4,-1.6) so one soil cell is exactly 2x2. Native AStar2D uses Manhattan distance and four-connected unit-cost edges; build in physics batches using layer 8 support, layer 1 capsule checks and the shared shadow footprint. Check mid-edge support and thin walls and replan after new obstacles. World owns area and clock integration. Run `tools/verify_npc_navigation.gd` and `tools/verify_npc_roster.gd` for route, collision and cast regressions.
- Dialogue uses visible nearby guests and streams at most two speakers per turn. Group turns can only retrieve prior speech known to their whole audience. An unsolicited topic-based contribution is limited to one per conversation. Retry only the failed speaker.
- NPCs keep four-connected Manhattan A* and skip intermediate grid points when the capsule, shadow and ground checks permit a straight segment. On contact, refresh nearby cells and replan from the actual body position on the same physics tick. Start a batched rebuild immediately if the local update cannot find a route. Retry after a short pause only when the rebuild finds no route. Keep the chosen endpoint until arrival or a schedule change. Dialogue streams DeepSeek SSE while paused and writes the completed reply to history after the stream's completion marker.
